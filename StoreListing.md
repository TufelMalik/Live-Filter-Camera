## 4. Privacy Policy (GDPR / CCPA / Play Store Compliant)

**Effective Date:** August 23, 2026  
**Developer:** TechQuantum  
**Contact:** support@techquantum.com  

### 1. Overview
Live Filter Camera ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our mobile application.

### 2. Permissions & Data Collection
Live Filter Camera requires certain device permissions solely to provide core camera and media functionality:
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**: Required to display the camera preview and capture photos/videos with real-time filters. Camera frames are processed entirely on-device and never transmitted to any server.
- **Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)**: Required to record audio when capturing video clips. Audio is saved locally with the video.
- **Storage / Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`)**: Required to save captured photos and videos to your device's `DCIM/LiveFilterCamera/` folder and display them in the in-app gallery.

### 3. On-Device Processing
All image processing, color lookups (3D LUTs), GLSL shaders, and video encoding are performed **100% locally on your device's GPU and CPU**. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your photos, videos, facial geometry, or personal data.

### 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Live Filter Camera does not incorporate third-party analytics trackers, ad networks, or data brokers that harvest personal identification.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

### 6. Updates to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
